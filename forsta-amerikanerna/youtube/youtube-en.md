The First Americans: how people reached the Americas, 40,000 years in 5 minutes

## Description
How and when did people reach the Americas? Follow 40,000 years on a globe. Seas fall and rise, the Bering land bridge forms and breaks, and ice sheets grow and melt. Finds in the ground and ancient genomes show where and when people lived.

Archaeology and genetics are shown side by side. Every event is marked:
- established (solid line),
- suggested (dashed),
- disputed (dotted).

That is why both the White Sands footprints and the 2026 dispute over Monte Verde appear.

About the content
The information was checked against sources on 21 November 2026, and the 88 sources are linked in the interactive version. The ice sheets are a simplified model, and the routes show readings, not exact paths. Many Indigenous peoples have their own histories of their origins; this video shows what archaeology and genetics suggest, which is one perspective among several.

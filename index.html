Interactive Pedagogicals

Interactive, teaching-oriented web pages that make research visible: press play, time runs, and the evidence appears where and when it belongs. No external libraries, fonts, images or audio files.

Interaktiva, pedagogiska webbsidor som gör forskning synlig: tryck på play, tiden löper och beläggen dyker upp där och när de hör hemma.

The First Americans / De första amerikanerna

How people reached the Americas, from the oldest traces the finds suggest to the year 1500. An interactive globe and timeline with archaeology and genetics side by side, 65 sourced events and a genetic family tree.
